2.0 PRO® vs. LeafGuard

LeafGuard's reverse-curve design has been around for over 100 years. It was innovative in its time — here's why more homeowners are choosing 2.0 PRO® instead.

LeafGuard Problems

Common issues with the reverse-curve design.

  • Large opening for debris — smaller leaves and grit can still get inside and clog the system.
  • Moss and algae growth — the dark, damp channel behind the curve is a breeding ground.
  • Water overshoot — in heavy downpours, water can simply overshoot the curve entirely.
  • Requires full gutter replacement — even when your current gutters are new.
  • Prone to freezing over — the curved design is known to build up ice in winter.

2.0 PRO®'s Advantages

Nothing compares to 2.0 PRO®.

  • 440-micron mesh — keeps out even the smallest debris particles, not just large leaves.
  • Triple-Rib™ design — slows water down as it glides over the screen, so it's captured instead of lost.
  • Installs on new or existing gutters — no forced replacement of gutters that are already sound.
  • 2.0 PRO HEAT™ available — keeps gutters fully operational through the coldest months.
  • Triple-Lifetime Warranty — materials, workmanship, and no-clog, fully transferable.

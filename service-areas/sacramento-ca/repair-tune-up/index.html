When Sacramento Gutters Need Repair, Not Replacement — Signs to Watch For

Sacramento's long, dry season bakes valley oak and sycamore leaf litter into a hardened crust, then atmospheric river storms can dump months of rain in a matter of days. In Sacramento, that usually shows up as sagging hangers, leaking seams, or gutters that no longer drain toward the downspout. A repair and tune-up fixes the specific problem instead of replacing gutters that still have years of life left in them.

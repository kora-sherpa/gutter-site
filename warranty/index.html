The Triple-Lifetime Warranty

Three separate lifetime guarantees — on materials, on workmanship, and on clogs — all fully transferable if you sell your home.

Lifetime Materials Warranty

The 2.0 PRO® frame and mesh are covered against manufacturing defects — including rust, corrosion, and UV breakdown — for as long as you own your home.

Lifetime Workmanship Warranty

Every installation performed by our certified technicians is covered against defects in the work itself — no fine print, no exceptions for normal use.

100% Clog-Free Guarantee

If your gutters ever clog with 2.0 PRO® installed, we send a technician to clear and correct it at no extra cost to you — for life.

Fully TransferableAll three warranties transfer to the next homeowner at no charge if you sell — a genuine selling point at listing time. This warranty does not void your existing roof manufacturer's warranty.

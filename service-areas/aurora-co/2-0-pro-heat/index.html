Do Aurora Roofs Need Ice-Dam Protection? Here's How 2.0 PRO HEAT™ Helps

Aurora's High Plains winters bring the same freeze-thaw swings that cause ice dams along the eaves, which is why 2.0 PRO HEAT™ is a popular add-on for Aurora roofs. 2.0 PRO HEAT™ integrates a self-regulating heat cable directly into the 2.0 PRO® guard system, so meltwater keeps moving through the downspout instead of refreezing at the eaves.

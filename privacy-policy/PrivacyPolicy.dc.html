1. Overview

Gutter Guard Experts® ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, request an estimate, or communicate with us by phone, text, or email, and how we use, share, and protect that information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, email address, and property address you provide through our estimate forms, booking flow, or by calling us.
  • Property details — property type, ZIP code, and service preferences relevant to your estimate request.
  • Communications — records of calls, texts, emails, and chat messages between you and our team.
  • Usage data — pages visited, device and browser type, and general location, collected automatically via cookies and similar technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to schedule and provide estimates and services, process warranty claims, communicate with you about your project, send promotional offers (such as the $75 Visa gift card program) where you have consented to receive them, improve our website and services, and comply with legal obligations.

4. SMS/Text Messaging Program (TCPA & A2P 10DLC Disclosure)

By submitting your phone number through our estimate or booking forms and checking the applicable consent box, you agree to receive calls and/or text messages (SMS/MMS) from Gutter Guard Experts® — including messages sent using an automatic telephone dialing system or prerecorded voice — regarding your estimate, appointment reminders, and related service updates. This may include messages sent through our registered Application-to-Person (A2P) 10DLC messaging campaign, in compliance with wireless carrier and Campaign Registry requirements.

  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase of any goods or services.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, or HELP for assistance.
  • You may also opt out by contacting us directly using the information in the Contact Us section below.
  • This program is governed by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and applicable carrier guidelines. [CONFIRM CURRENT A2P 10DLC CAMPAIGN REGISTRATION DETAILS AND CARRIER-REQUIRED LANGUAGE WITH COUNSEL]

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our site, and measure the performance of our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect how parts of our site function.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (such as scheduling, financing partners, and marketing platforms), with financing partners you choose to apply through, and when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

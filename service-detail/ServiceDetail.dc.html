Built to Outperform, Engineered to Last

When it comes to protecting your home from water damage, not all gutter guards are engineered equal. Our 2.0 PRO® Guard Installation service is built around a single patented system — designed, tested, and manufactured to outperform the plastic and thin-aluminum guards sold by big-box retailers and national franchises alike.

Every bracket and hanger is inspected and re-secured before the guard ever goes on.

Every 2.0 PRO® guard starts with a frame of aircraft-quality extruded aluminum, tempered to a T5 finish and rated to a tensile strength of over 20,000 PSI — up to 10 times stronger than the standard stamped aluminum used across the industry. Unlike thinner frames that warp and bow as temperatures swing between summer heat and winter freeze, T5 aluminum holds its shape and its seal against your existing gutter for the life of the system.

Sitting atop that frame is a 440-micron, surgical-grade stainless-steel mesh — fine enough to stop pine needles, asphalt shingle grit, and wind-blown sand, but engineered with our patented Triple-Rib™ profile to keep water moving. As rain hits the stepped ribs, it slows on contact and is pulled downward by capillary action instead of sheeting off the edge, capturing over 40% more water than a flat-mesh competitor during heavy storms.

The guard itself is installed at an angle close to your roof's own pitch, so wind as light as 10–15 mph naturally clears leaves and debris off the surface before they ever have a chance to build up — which is what makes the system effectively self-cleaning for the life of your gutters.

Installation starts the same way every job does at Gutter Guard Experts®: a full gutter and downspout cleaning, because 2.0 PRO® should only ever go on top of a system that's already flowing correctly. From there, our certified technicians re-secure loose hangers, correct pitch where needed, and seal every corner and end cap with a 50-year tri-polymer sealant before the guards are custom-fit and fastened into place — on new construction or your existing gutters, on virtually any roof type.

Every installation is backed by our Triple-Lifetime Warranty: a lifetime guarantee on materials, a lifetime guarantee on workmanship, and a 100% no-clog guarantee, all fully transferable if you sell your home. Combined with our optional 2.0 PRO HEAT™ ice-melt add-on for winter climates, 2.0 PRO® is designed to be the only gutter protection decision you'll ever have to make.

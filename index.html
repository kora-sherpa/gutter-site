The Engineering

Why 2.0 PRO® Technology Wins

Three years of prototyping, seven design iterations, and one U.S. patent went into a gutter guard built to actually outperform — not just look the part.

Patented Triple-Rib™ Water Siphoning

Three stepped ribs slow rainfall on contact and create capillary action that pulls water down into the gutter — capturing over 40% more water than a flat-mesh guard, even in downpours.

Surgical 440-Micron Stainless Mesh

A woven micro-filter fine enough to stop pine needles, roof grit and wind-blown sand — yet open enough to keep water moving. It won't clog, rust, corrode or break down under UV.

Aircraft-Grade T5 Aluminum Frame

Extruded and tempered to a T5 finish with a tensile strength over 20,000 PSI — up to 10x stronger than the thin stamped aluminum in most gutter guards. It won't warp, bow or separate.

Angled Self-Cleaning Pitch

Installed at nearly the same pitch as your roofline, so leaves and debris slide off in as little as 10–15 mph of wind. Greatly reduced — often eliminated — maintenance.

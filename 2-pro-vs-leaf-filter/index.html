2.0 PRO® vs. LeafFilter

Two gutter guard systems, two very different approaches. Here's how our patented aluminum-and-steel system compares to LeafFilter's plastic-framed design.

Common LeafFilter Problems

Based on the plastic-and-mesh construction used across the industry.

  • Warping plastic frame — expands and contracts with hot and cold temperature swings.
  • Flat design collects debris — sits relatively flat in the gutter and acts like a shelf.
  • Screen separation — the plastic frame and stainless mesh expand at different rates and can pull apart.
  • Water overshoots in heavy rain — mesh size and construction let rain slide over the edge.
  • Snow and ice buildup — no integrated solution for winter climates.
  • No lifetime warranty on materials or workmanship.

2.0 PRO®'s Advantages

A single patented system, engineered as one unit.

  • Aircraft-grade aluminum frame — holds its shape through every season, no warping.
  • Angled installation — prevents leaves, pine needles, and debris from building up on top.
  • One fused system — frame and mesh engineered together, so there's no separation to worry about.
  • Triple-Rib™ design — slows water as it hits the screen, capturing over 40% more in heavy storms.
  • 2.0 PRO HEAT™ available — optional ice-melt add-on engineered for winter climates.
  • Triple-Lifetime Warranty — materials, workmanship, and no-clog, fully transferable.
